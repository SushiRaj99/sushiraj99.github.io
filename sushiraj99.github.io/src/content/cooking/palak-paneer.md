## Ingredients

- 37g black lentils, dry
- ½ block Patel Brothers low-fat paneer, cubed
- ¼ cup water (for blending)
- 1½ tbsp avocado oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 green chilis
- ½ large onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- ½ green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 packed cup Trader Joe's Power to the Greens, chopped
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp red chili powder
- ½ tsp salt

## Method

1. **Cook the lentils** — Rinse the black lentils and simmer in water until tender (~25-30 min).
2. **Blend the paneer** — Cube the paneer and blend with water until smooth.
3. **Sauté chilis and onion** — Heat half the avocado oil in a pan. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter, then add the green chilis, onion, and ginger garlic paste and sauté until the onions start to brown (~5 min).
4. **Add the bell pepper** — Add the bell pepper and continue sautéing until browning (~4 min).
5. **Add the spices** — Stir in the turmeric, garam masala, red chili powder, and salt and continue sautéing.
6. **Add the tomato** — Drop in the chopped tomato and sauté until it's melted down and everything has blended together (~5 min).
7. **Finish and simmer** — Stir in the power greens, lentils, and blended paneer. Combine well and let it simmer, stirring occasionally (~10 min).
8. **Munch Munch Munch** - I usually like to eat this with whole wheat wraps, but also goes well with parota or rice.
