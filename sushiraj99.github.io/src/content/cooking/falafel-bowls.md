## Ingredients

- 37g black lentils, dry
- 18g red lentils, dry
- 6 pieces Trader Joe's Mediterranean falafel (air-fried)
  - Trader Joes also has a Dill Pickle Falafel that I really like
- 1 packed cup Trader Joe's Power Greens
- ½ large green pepper, sliced
- ½ large yellow onion, diced
- 2 green chilis, sliced
- 1 tomato
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 Trader Joe's Organic Whole Wheat Wraps, torn into pieces for scooping
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- ½ tbsp red chili powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ large avocado
- 1 cup low-fat (1%) cottage cheese

## Method

1. **Cook the Lentils** - Rinse the black and red lentils together, then simmer in water until tender (~25 - 30 min).
2. **Air Fry the Falafel** - I usually do 8 minutes at 350°F
3. **Fry the Veggies** - Start with the oil, onions and green chilis and half of the salt and fry till the onions start turning brown, then drop in the green peppers and fry until the peppers start to brown
4. **Season** - Stir in the turmeric, red chili powder, and salt with the sautéed vegetables, cooked lentils, and cooked & crushed falafel
5. **Cheeeese** - Drop in the cottage cheese and stir well
6. **More Greens & a Tomato!** - Chop up the greens and drop them in along with the tomato. Keep stirring/flying until the tomato has fully blended into the mix
7. **Assemble** - Very lightly air fry the torn up wraps to add some crisp. Then top the mix made with the pieces and the avacado (I usually also throw in some spicy ranch)
