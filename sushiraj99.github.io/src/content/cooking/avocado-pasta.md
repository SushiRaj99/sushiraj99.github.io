## Ingredients

- 1 large avocado
- 1¼ cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 2 green chilis
- ¼ cup water (for the sauce)
- 37g black lentils, dry
- ½ block Trader Joe's High Protein Organic Tofu (air-fried)
- 1½ cups Trader Joe's Chickpea Fusilli Pasta, dry
- 1 packed cup Trader Joe's Power to the Greens
- ½ large onion, sliced
- ¼ cup olives
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. **Cook the lentils** — Rinse the black lentils and cook (~20 mins).
2. **Air-fry the tofu** — Cut the tofu into thin strips and air fry (I usually do 8 minutes at 350°F)
3. **Cook the pasta** — Cook the chickpea fusilli according to package instructions (~8 min).
4. **Make the avocado sauce** — Blend the avocado, cottage cheese, and half the salt
5. **Sauté the onion and greens** — Heat the avocado oil in a pan. Start by frying the onions and green chilis with the ginger garlic paste until the onions start to brown. Then add the lentils, tofu and the remaining salt and continue frying for another 10 minuts.
6. **Combine and serve** — Throw in the avacado sauce and the olives and keep the stove on until it's all blended together
7. **Nom Nom Nom** - Eat your green pasta. I usually like to throw in a little garlic bread on the side
