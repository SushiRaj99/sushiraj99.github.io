I didn't have cornhole boards for a friend to borrow for a party, so I decided to try making some. The requests I got were colors yellow and green, spring themed. I started by mocking everything up in Photoshop, then found all the materials I needed between Facebook Marketplace, Amazon, and Home Depot. For anyone wondering, always try Facebook Marketplace for finding wood — I probably saved myself at least $75. After getting the wood, I used a planer to smooth it out and a hole saw to cut the 6-inch hole. Then I spray painted it and ran it through a laser engraver to engrave daisies into the wood. Three coats of polyurethane later (with sanding between coats), done!

**Lessons learned:** use the slow speed on a hole saw (RIP my wrists), and polyurethane does not come off your hands easily — never forget gloves.
